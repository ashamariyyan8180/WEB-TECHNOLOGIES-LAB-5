About Our University

Education for a Brighter Future

The University of Layyah is a public-sector university in Layyah, Punjab, Pakistan. It provides opportunities for students to pursue higher education across different academic disciplines.

University life encourages students to develop their knowledge, practical skills, creativity, and research abilities to prepare for future challenges.
